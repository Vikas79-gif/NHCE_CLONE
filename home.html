About NHCE

Our Legacy of Excellence

For over 55 years, New Horizon Educational Institutions Group has been at the forefront of technical education

Our Mission

To provide quality technical education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values to become innovative leaders and responsible citizens in a globalized world. We are committed to fostering an environment of academic excellence, research, and holistic development.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier institution of technical education and research, known for producing competent engineers and managers who contribute meaningfully to society and drive technological advancement globally.
